Box anatomy

The six details we ask for on every box order. Send all of them in your first message.

L

Length

The longer side of the box opening, measured on the inside.

e.g. L = 12 in (305 mm)

W

Width

The shorter side of the box opening, also measured on the inside.

e.g. W = 8 in (203 mm)

H

Height

From the inside bottom of the box up to the fold line of the flaps.

e.g. H = 6 in (152 mm)

Always give sizes in this order: L × W × H.

F

Flaps / closure

How the box closes: four flaps sealed with tape, a tuck-in lid, a mailer with a lid, or a sleeve.

Not sure? Send us a photo of a box you like.

P

Print panels

How many sides are printed (1, 2, all 4, or flaps too) and whether it's full color or one color.

e.g. Front + back, full color; sides plain

B

Board / material

How heavy is what goes inside? We use this to choose single wall or double wall corrugated, or paperboard.

e.g. Contents: 5 kg of clothes · Single wall

tip:Measure the inside of the box, not the outside. Inches or millimeters are both fine, just tell us which.

For every order

Four things we need before we can give a final quote, whatever the product.

01Required

Print-ready artwork

We don't offer layout or design, so we need the final file. PDF or AI, text outlined, 300 dpi, CMYK, with 3 mm bleed.

02

Quantity

How many pieces per design, and how many designs. 50 pcs and 500 pcs are priced differently.

03

Target date

When you need it. Tell us right away if it's a rush so we can check if it's doable.

04

Pickup or delivery

Will you pick up at our remote shop in Tondo, Manila, or should we ship? For delivery, include the location.

Checklist by product

Find your product and prepare the details listed under it.

Labels & stickers

  • SizeWidth × height, e.g. 50 × 80 mm
  • ShapeRectangle, circle, oval, or custom die-cut
  • MaterialPaper, vinyl / waterproof, or transparent
  • FinishMatte or glossy
  • Roll or sheetRoll for labeling machines or large volumes
  • Where it goesBottle, jar, pouch, fridge or freezer?

Sleeves

  • Product size, L × W × HSize of the box or item the sleeve wraps
  • Paper or boardThickness you want, or send a sample
  • FinishMatte or glossy
  • Printed sidesOutside only, or inside too
  • QuantityPer design

Hang tags

  • Size and shapee.g. 2 × 3.5 in, rectangle or die-cut
  • Paper thicknessRegular card or thick card
  • Print sidesFront only, or front and back
  • Hole and stringHole only, or with string attached
  • FinishMatte or glossy

Small boxes

  • Inside size, L × W × HSee the box anatomy above
  • Box styleTuck-end, mailer, or lid and base
  • BoardPaperboard, kraft, or thin corrugated
  • Print panelsOutside only, or inside too
  • Sample or dielineA photo of a sample, or a dieline file

Corrugated boxes

  • Inside size, L × W × HMeasured on the inside
  • Contents and weightWhat goes inside and roughly how many kilos
  • Wall typeSingle wall or double wall
  • Plain or printedIf printed, which sides and colors
  • QuantityTotal pieces

Big printed boxes

  • Inside size, L × W × HIn inches or mm
  • Contents and weightSo we pick the right board
  • Print panels and colorWhich sides, one color or full color
  • QuantitySmall runs, 1 to 100 pcs

Notebooks & magazines

  • Finished sizee.g. A5, A4, or custom
  • Page countTotal pages, including cover
  • PaperCover and inside pages
  • BindingStapled, glued (perfect bound), or spiral
  • Inside printFull color, black only, or blank / ruled
