Before you request a quote

Get to know your box first.

These are the six details we ask for on every box order. Include all of them in your first message and we can send you a faster, more accurate quote.

FIG. 01 · SAMPLE PRINTED BOX TAP A PART →

  • The longer side of the box opening, measured on the inside.

    e.g. L = 12 in (305 mm)

  • The shorter side of the box opening, also measured on the inside.

    e.g. W = 8 in (203 mm)

  • From the inside bottom of the box up to the fold line of the flaps.

    e.g. H = 6 in (152 mm)

    Always give sizes in this order: L × W × H.

  • How the box closes: standard four flaps sealed with tape, a tuck-in lid, a mailer with a lid, or a sleeve.

    Not sure? Send us a photo of a box you like.

  • Tell us how many sides are printed (1 side, 2 sides, all 4 sides, or flaps too) and whether it's full color or one color.

    e.g. Front + back, full color; sides plain

  • How heavy is what goes inside? We use this to choose single wall or double wall corrugated, or paperboard for small boxes.

    e.g. Contents: 5 kg of clothes · Single wall

Measure the inside of the box, not the outside. Inches or millimeters are both fine, just tell us which.

For every order

Four things we need before we can give a final quote, whatever the product.

01Required

Print-ready artwork

We don't offer layout or design, so we need the final file. PDF or AI, text outlined, 300 dpi, CMYK, with 3 mm bleed.

02

Quantity

How many pieces per design, and how many designs. 50 pcs and 500 pcs are priced differently.

03

Target date

When you need it. Tell us right away if it's a rush so we can check if it's doable.

04

Pickup or delivery

Will you pick up at our shop in Rodriguez, or should we ship? For delivery, include the location.
